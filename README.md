# Nicole Saab Carter's Billing Tracker

A personal monthly work hours tracker — no login, no backend, just open and use.

**[Open the tracker →](https://brandon728.github.io/work-hours-tracker/)**

## What it does

- Track planned and actual hours for every workday in a month
- See totals, remaining hours, and daily average needed to hit your goal
- Earn achievement badges as you log hours throughout the month
- View a chart of your progress over the month
- Browse history across all past months

## How data is saved

Everything is saved automatically to your browser's local storage as you type — no Save button needed. Use the same browser on the same device every time to keep your data.
